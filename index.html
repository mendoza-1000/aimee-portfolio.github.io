Case 01

Localizing a foreign iGaming brand in Mexico

The Challenge

Localize and help launch a foreign iGaming brand in Mexico across social media, email, and SMS with no initial paid media strategy.

The Goals

  • Successfully launch the digital presence, making it sound native to players.
  • Grow audience and engagement organically across X, Facebook (Pages & Groups), Instagram, Telegram, WhatsApp Channels, Threads & TikTok while complying with platform policies.
  • Establish the fundamentals to streamline production between freelancers and in-house departments on another continent.
  • Ensure compliance in every post to obtain SEGOB’s license approval and ensure annual renewal.
  • Position the brand as a reference in the Mexican iGaming industry long-term.

The Process · Research

The Research. I led the research on regulation and competitors. Based on this, I defined the strategy and the brand's voice.

Our Voice. The SWOT, values, audience, and objectives led to a voice that is “cool but not arrogant, relaxed but committed,” and a content strategy by platform.

The Strategy

  • Community Building: platform-native formats (meme-style Reels, polls, quizzes). Human-led interaction. Real-time posts.
  • Social: calendars in Monday/Google Sheets, published via Metricool/Hootsuite.
  • Email & SMS: Mexican Spanish copy for promotional briefs, suggesting Mexican dates and moments.

Influencer content was executed in a second phase, once the brand was organically established.

Case 01 · Analytics & automation

The Tracking

Planning and publishing content is easy, but optimizing it based on data is another story. Most platforms provide native analytics, but WhatsApp Channels' analytics are rudimentary. This is why I built this tracker. It turned a 2-hour month-end reporting task into a 5-minute job, allowing me to evaluate content types, formats, and topics to grow and keep the community active organically.

01

Daily_Followers

Daily log. Total followers, typed in from Insights. Net daily change and month are calculated.

02

Posts_Engagement

Every post. Date, category, reactions, forwards, tracked link. Engagement rate is calculated per post.

03

Monthly_Snapshot

Monthly freeze. Reach, growth and top region copied before WhatsApp overwrites its 30-day window.

04

Dashboard

One cell. Type a month and everything updates: growth, average engagement, top posts, latest snapshot.

Data Flow

WhatsApp Insights→Typed by hand, daily→Daily log→Monthly snapshot, typed→Dashboard, calculated
Before · 2h+

At the end of the month, I opened every post one by one (3–4 a day, about 30 days) and pulled the stats by hand.

After · 5 min

Each post is logged as it goes live. At month end, I type the month into one cell and the report is ready.

Case 02

Postales Literarias: creating a literary community outside the academy

The Challenge

Organize a short-story contest in postcard format to unite the UNAM Faculty of Arts community, the Design Faculty (to help students complete their social service through illustration), and high schools (CCHs). Foster critical reflection on utopias/dystopias through themes like political bodies, state violence, wars, ecology, and technology. Generate a plural space for critical literary creation, making young writers/illustrators visible.

The Personal Challenge. Collaborate successfully with the Center for Documentation and Diffusion of Critical Philosophy (FFyL/UNAM) while being a student at UAM Iztapalapa, to expand the project's reach compared to previous editions.

The Process

Institutional & Bureaucratic. Secured sponsorships from private and academic organizations, managed fund collection, and ensured compliance with institutional guidelines.

Creative & Networking. Edited and judged content that met the rules, so final judges (academics from UNAM, Siglo XXI, etc.) could give their verdict.

Outreach. Made the short-story booklet available to the non-university public in UNAM and independent bookstores in Mexico City for the first time.

Case 03

Copy creation & localization for Fintechs

The Challenge

Two Fintech brands (Bitso & RappiCard) wanted to land in the Mexican market, offering tailored options (credit cards, loans, apps, crypto) but their global strategy couldn't be replicated. It had to be localized according to the legal framework, engaging, and convert on SMS, email, in-app push, social, or website campaigns.

The Process

Localization at Persado. We created variants based on specific emotions while strictly following legal guidelines: no alarmist language, no guaranteed earnings, no absolute/unrealistic terms, and no confusing users about product terms.

Variants by emotion

Initiation
Safety
Encouragement
Curiosity
Excitement
Urgency

Each variant combined emotions inside the legal guardrails; the best performer reaches the client.

Case 03 · The Results
Bitso

CTRL: “We help you discover how to make the most of cryptocurrencies.”

“¿Nuevo? Te ayudamos a descubrir cómo sacarle partido a las criptomonedas. Comienza a usar Bitso.”
Variant 1 · Initiation, safety, encouragement
“{Name}, descubre cómo sacarle partido a las criptomonedas con Bitso ¡Empieza hoy mismo!”
Variant 2 · Curiosity, excitement, urgency
The Localization Win. “Make the most” became “sacarle partido” (natural Mexican Spanish), and I caught a fact error (35 vs. 36 cryptocurrencies) during QA.
RappiCard

CTRL: “We offer a card with cashback and other benefits for the users.”

“La tarjeta que se mueve contigo te consiente con un 5% de cashback y muchos beneficios ¡Disfrútala!”
Variant 1 · Exclusivity, gratification, encouragement
“Tu RappiCard será tu mejor compañera porque te ofrece hasta un 5% de cashback y más ventajas ¡Pídela hoy!”
Variant 2 · Exclusivity, excitement, urgency
The Localization Win. Localized the idea of offering a card without using the verb “ofrecer” (using “la tarjeta que se mueve contigo”).

Case 04

Copy creation & localization for Age-Restricted Products

The Challenge

A highly regulated campaign for a tobacco brand (IQOS) that wanted to reach the Mexican market, following local laws, avoiding banned terms (“cigarro electrónico”, “vaping”, “vape”), and building a community around the product.

5
campaigns
8
variants

The Process

Client’s Brief. Adapt a referral campaign for adult smokers in Mexico across email and printed billboards. Handle 8 variants across 5 campaigns, checked for compliance and scored for engagement against prior tests with similar brands.

Legal Framework. We cannot use banned terms to describe the product. We must keep the idea of inviting a smoker to join an exclusive team of non-traditional smokers and improve their lifestyle by smelling less like tobacco.

Case 05

Editorial: Building a literary catalog in a new market

The Challenge

In 2018, the Mexican ebook market was still in its infancy. The challenge was to build a valuable, original catalog from scratch in record time, while establishing Literalia's identity as a benchmark in the Mexican digital publishing landscape.

The Process

As Co-Editor in Chief, I led the acquisition and editing of 14 books by new Mexican fiction writers (novel, short story, flash fiction, poetry). We secured publishing contracts with all 14 authors, curated their work, and created a style manual for editorial processes in Latin American and Mexican Spanish.

14 titles by new Mexican writers across novel, short story, flash fiction and poetry, 2018-2019.
